## Abstract

Here I present a low cost UHF RFID Inventory System. Built around the RodinBell M300 Series RFID Development Sample (resold as generic R200 or R300 boards on [aliexpress](https://www.aliexpress.us/w/wholesale-uhf-rfid-reader-r200.html?spm=a2g0o.productlist.search.0)), this self contained device is capable of reading UHF RFID tags from 10-20 feet away, even when they are occluded by other objects. This makes it ideal for detecting "is this item in Emma's car?" for the purposes of tracking and inventorying my work tools.
